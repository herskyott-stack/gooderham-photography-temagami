A family eye on Lake Temagami since 1901.

Photographer Gerry Gooderham works from Island 843 on Lake Temagami. His grandfather Gordon Stewart Gooderham first came to the lake in 1901 and founded Camp Chimo in 1926 — a heritage carried into portraits, fine-art landscapes and living-history work today.
